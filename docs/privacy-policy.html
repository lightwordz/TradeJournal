Privacy Policy — TradeJournal

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Package: com.aistudio.tradejournal.kxmpzq

1. Overview & Offline-First Architecture

TradeJournal is an offline-first personal trading journal for Indian equity, F&O, and commodity traders. We do not require account sign-in, do not operate remote servers, and do not collect, transmit, rent, or sell your personal or financial data.

2. Data You Enter & Purpose (DPDP Act, 2023)

To function as your personal trading ledger, TradeJournal processes the following data locally on your device:

  • Profile & risk settings (display name, starting capital, daily loss threshold, broker preset).
  • Trade logs, timestamps, order prices, capital deposit/withdrawal notes, strategy tags, and mood/rating entries.
  • Optional trade chart screenshots chosen via Android Photo Picker and saved inside app-private storage.

3. Storage Location, Backup & Retention

All records remain exclusively inside your Android device's private SQLite database (Room) and app-private files folder. Unencrypted cloud backups of the trade database and screenshots are disabled in Android backup extraction rules. Data is retained only until you delete it or uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Permissions

  • No ads or trackers: Zero advertising IDs, analytics trackers, or social SDKs are present.
  • Android Photo Picker & Storage Access Framework: Grants one-time access strictly to the image or CSV file you pick. No broad storage, camera, microphone, or location permissions are requested.
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition (On-Device): Reads text locally from screenshots you attach to pre-fill order numbers without uploading screenshots to our servers.

5. Your Rights & Data Erasure

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you have full control to access, correct, export (Settings → Export Trades CSV), and erase your records at any time using Settings → Delete All Local Data or by uninstalling TradeJournal.

6. Financial Disclaimer

Record-keeping tool. Not investment advice. Not SEBI-registered. Calculations are estimates; verify with your broker's contract note. TradeJournal does not execute trades or hold funds; CSV import is strictly read-only, and any future broker import will also be strictly read-only. TradeJournal is independent and not affiliated with NSE, BSE, MCX, Zerodha, Upstox, Groww, or Angel One.
