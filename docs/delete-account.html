Account & Data Deletion — TradeJournal

No server accounts exist: TradeJournal stores all trades, capital logs, settings, and screenshots strictly locally on your Android device. No copy of your trading journal exists on external servers.

How to Delete All Your Data Immediately

  1. Open TradeJournal → Settings.
  2. Optional: Tap Export Trades (CSV) if you want a local copy first.
  3. Tap Delete All Local Data and confirm. This permanently deletes all trade logs, capital entries, and saved chart screenshots from app storage.
  4. Uninstalling TradeJournal or clearing app storage in Android Settings also removes 100% of remaining preferences.
