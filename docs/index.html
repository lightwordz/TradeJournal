About TradeJournal

TradeJournal helps individuals record their executed equity, F&O, and MCX trades locally on their device, estimate brokerage and statutory taxes, review calendar activity, and export personal CSV records.

Disclaimer: Record-keeping tool. Not investment advice. Not SEBI-registered. Calculations are estimates; verify with your broker's contract note. Independent app — not affiliated with NSE, BSE, MCX, Zerodha, Upstox, Groww, or Angel One.
